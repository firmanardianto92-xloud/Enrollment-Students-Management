Family ID Blueprint

How to grow the Enrollment Desk we built into one school system for the whole foundation. Everyone involved (prospective students, parents, students, siblings, teachers, staff and the foundation) is connected through one family identity, so every enrollment becomes the start of a long relationship with that family.

1. The family lifecycle

The five stages from the DH Family ID slide, mapped to system modules and the people who use them. The Enrollment Desk already covers Attract and Enroll.

01Progression

A student moves up to the next school level within the same foundation.

Trigger
First-semester report card in Grade 6 or 9, or grades high enough for the acceleration track
In system
A lead is created automatically in the Enrollment Desk with source Internal progression and data already filled in

02Family Expansion

A younger or older sibling enrolls too.

Trigger
Siblings are recorded with a date of birth. 12 months before a sibling reaches kindergarten or primary age, the family is invited to Open House
In system
Lead with source Sibling; the sibling discount is applied automatically

03Advocacy

Families recommend the school to other families.

Trigger
After one semester and a high satisfaction score, the family is invited to give a testimonial and receives a referral code
In system
Counted under the existing Parent Referral source on the dashboard

2. System architecture

Six layers from top to bottom. Each role gets its own simple entry point, but all of them share one family login, one set of services and one database. Systems the school already uses are connected, not thrown away.

3. Family ID in practice

An example family (fictional data). Each member has their own role and stage, all tied to one family number.

4. Experience by role

What each person sees when they first sign in, the three things they do most often, and what changes compared with how things work today.

Prospective students & parents

Home Portal

Home: programs, facilities, student activities, Open House dates.

Main tasks
  • Register for Open House with one short form
  • Track application status, like tracking a parcel
  • Pay for the form and choose a test date

What changes: no need to keep asking for status updates over WhatsApp.

Parents of students

Family App

Home: all children on one screen, with attendance, assignments, bills and announcements.

Main tasks
  • Pay school fees for all children in one bill
  • Follow progress and report cards
  • Report sick leave, message the homeroom teacher, enroll a sibling

What changes: one inbox replaces many WhatsApp groups, and family data is already filled in when enrolling a sibling.

Students

Student space

Home: today's schedule, assignments and activities.

Main tasks
  • Open learning materials and submit assignments
  • Join class and group discussions
  • Sign up for clubs and record achievements

What changes: an achievement portfolio builds up until graduation and supports recommendations for acceleration or the next school level.

Teachers

Teacher Workspace

Home: today's classes, work to grade, students who need attention.

Main tasks
  • Enter attendance and grades once
  • Share materials and lead discussions
  • Message parents

What changes: grades flow straight into report cards and the Family App, with no re-compiling in spreadsheets.

Operations staff

Staff Back-office

Home by unit: admissions, finance, administration or facilities.

Main tasks
  • Admissions in the Enrollment Desk already built
  • Billing and automatic virtual-account payment matching
  • Manage room and facility bookings

What changes: enrolled applicants become student records in the SIS without the admin office retyping them.

Foundation

Foundation Dashboard

Home: a summary across all units and school levels.

What they monitor
  • Enrollment, retention and progression per level
  • Share of families with more than one child, and referral results
  • Long-term value per family and facility usage

What changes: decisions come from data across units, not from separate reports by each school.

6. Experience principles

One login per family

Parents switch between children with one tap. Students have their own login with a more limited view.

WhatsApp for updates, portal for details

Notifications go out on WhatsApp because that is where parents are active. The link opens the right page in the Family App.

Enter once, use everywhere

Admission data flows into the SIS, billing and report cards. Teachers and admin staff do not retype it.

Show what each role needs

Students do not see bills, teachers do not see finance data, and the foundation sees aggregate figures, not personal data.

Protect children's privacy

Parent consent is recorded in line with Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 27 of 2022). Student photos appear on the public portal only with permission.

Language parents understand

Statuses read "Waiting for test results", not codes. Screens are designed for phones first.

8. Decisions needed before implementation

  1. Where do we start? I recommend Phase 1 (Home Portal, Open House form and status check), because it connects directly to the existing Enrollment Desk.
  2. Which systems are in use today? For example the SIS, finance software, LMS or attendance system. This decides what gets connected and what gets built new.
  3. Which units sit under the foundation? Kindergarten, primary, junior high, senior high or boarding. This decides the level structure in the portal and dashboards.
  4. Branding: keep a fictional name for the mock-up, or use the school's official name and logo?

This document is a proposal for discussion. Family names, figures and timelines are examples.
